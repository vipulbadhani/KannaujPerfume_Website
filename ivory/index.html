From flower
to attar.

Kannauj still distils the old way — and so do we. It takes a full night over a slow fire, and a year of patience, to make a single batch.

01

Plucked at dawn

Petals are gathered before 7am, while the dew still holds their scent.

02

The copper deg

Flowers and water are sealed with clay in a copper still over a wood fire.

03

The bhapka

Fragrant steam travels by bamboo pipe into sandalwood oil, cooled in water.

04

Aged in kuppi

The attar rests in camel-leather kuppis for months until it is truly round.
