Questions, answered

What is the difference between attar and eau de parfum?

Attar is a pure perfume oil with no alcohol — it sits close to the skin and lasts a very long time. Our eaux de parfum carry the same essences in perfumer's alcohol, for a lighter, more radiant trail.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship from Kannauj within 48 hours and arrive in 2–5 working days across India. Shipping is free and insured.

Can I return a fragrance?

Unopened bottles can be returned within 14 days. Not sure what to choose? Start with our discovery samples.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to most countries. Duties and delivery times are shown at checkout.
